What the prototype does

Post it once. It works through the list for you.

  1. Slot opens. Staff post the service, stylist and time.
  2. Best match gets the offer: right service, stylist and availability, earliest sign-up first.
  3. They have 15 minutes to tap Yes or No.
  4. Yes = booked. No or no reply = next person, automatically.
If nobody accepts, the opening is marked Unfilled2 / 5

How it handles things going wrong

The problems you described, handled automatically

"Depends on whether we remember to follow up"

It waits for each reply and moves on by itself after 15 minutes.

"Competing acceptances caused confusion"

Only one client can get the slot. A late "yes" is told it's no longer available.

"We lose track of who's next"

Every offer is recorded. If the computer restarts mid-offer, it picks up where it left off, and failed texts are retried.

Being clear about the prototype

What's real, what's simulated, what's left out

Working now

  • Matching and earliest-first order
  • One offer at a time, 15-minute reply window
  • Automatic move to the next person
  • No double-booking; late replies told it's taken
  • Stop / cancel controls and live status
  • Staff adding clients to the waitlist

Simulated

  • Text messages: shown as an on-screen page, not sent to real phones
  • Square: cancellations are entered by hand; the booking is a pretend confirmation
  • Waitlist: sample clients instead of your Google Sheet; resets when the app restarts

Not included yet

  • Clients replying "YES" by text
  • Staff logins
  • Reports on filled vs. empty slots
  • Rules like "only if 2+ hours' notice"
Everything runs on a local computer; no real client data was used4 / 5
